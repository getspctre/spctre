---
title: Simulation guidance
description: Evidence-led, inline policy-review guidance for policy replay outcomes.
---

**Simulation guidance** is shown directly beside replay outcomes. It highlights
the policy references most associated with changed sampled outcomes, while
people retain decision authority. It is governed by the
`spctre-agent-governance-v1` policy pack.

## Agents vs. simulation guidance

| | Agents | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Your code / external frameworks | Spctre |
| Purpose | Take actions (charge, transfer, call APIs) | Highlight simulation evidence for review (never act) |
| Governance pack | Your custom policies | `spctre-agent-governance-v1` |
| Operations attribution | Standard evidence | `SIMULATION_GUIDANCE` |
| Can approve reviews | Yes (with policy ALLOW) | **No** — a reviewer must decide |
| Can publish policies | Yes (with policy ALLOW) | **No** — a reviewer must publish |

## What simulation guidance does

- Interprets simulation outcome changes before a policy is published
- Highlights the policy references connected to newly denied or allowed events
- Records a reviewer’s disposition and rationale in the audit ledger

Governance posture, escalation claiming, and pending-approval status are shown
in their relevant workflow pages. They are not simulation guidance.

## What simulation guidance cannot do

The **Simulation Guidance Governance Pack** (`spctre-agent-governance-v1`, connector: `spctre-agent`) preserves human decision authority for:

- Cannot approve or reject review queue items
- Cannot publish or mutate policy branches
- Cannot resolve escalations
- Cannot issue credentials or tokens

All recorded guidance requires a named human reviewer. The reviewer's rationale is stored in the audit trail.

## Governance rules

The governance pack includes rules that enforce:

- Named system principal on every recorded guidance action
- `SIMULATION_GUIDANCE` attribution in the operations log
- Human reviewer rationale required when recording, editing, or dismissing guidance
- Blocks on self-approval, self-publish, and self-resolution

The `spctre-agent.*` stable-rule-ID namespace is reserved for Spctre-managed
guidance controls. Customer policy must use its own namespace, such as
`acme.advisor.*`.

## Guidance and audit

Simulation guidance operates within the **Policy Replay** page. Recorded reviewer
decisions are appended to the workspace's **Audit Ledger**.
