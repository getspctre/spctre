---
title: Configuring SSO
description: OIDC and SAML setup walkthrough in the UI, testing the login flow, and JIT provisioning.
---

Spctre supports OIDC and SAML 2.0 for single sign-on. Once configured, users sign in through your identity provider (IdP) instead of Spctre-managed credentials.

## Before you start

You'll need:
- Admin access in Spctre and in your IdP
- Your IdP's metadata URL (SAML) or discovery endpoint (OIDC)
- Permission to create an application in your IdP

## Opening SSO settings

Go to **Admin → Authentication → SSO**.

## OIDC setup

<Steps>
<Step>

Click **Add provider → OIDC**.

</Step>
<Step>

In your IdP, create a new OIDC application. Set:
- **Redirect URI**: shown in the Spctre OIDC setup form (typically `https://your-control-plane.example.com/api/auth/callback/oidc`)
- **Scopes**: `openid`, `email`, `profile`

</Step>
<Step>

Back in Spctre, fill in:
- **Discovery URL** — your IdP's OIDC discovery endpoint (e.g. `https://your-idp.example.com/.well-known/openid-configuration`)
- **Client ID** — from your IdP application
- **Client secret** — from your IdP application

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Save and test**. Spctre opens a test login flow in a new tab. Sign in with your IdP credentials. If the test passes, the SSO configuration is active.

</Step>
</Steps>

## SAML setup

<Steps>
<Step>

Click **Add provider → SAML**.

</Step>
<Step>

Spctre shows:
- **SP Entity ID** — the service provider identifier to register in your IdP
- **ACS URL** — the assertion consumer service URL your IdP posts to

Register these in your IdP's SAML application configuration.

</Step>
<Step>

From your IdP, copy:
- **IdP metadata URL** (preferred) or paste the metadata XML directly

</Step>
<Step>

Paste the metadata URL into Spctre and click **Import metadata**. Spctre reads the certificate and endpoint URLs.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Save and test**. Complete a test login to verify.

</Step>
</Steps>

## JIT provisioning

Just-in-time provisioning creates a Spctre account automatically when a user successfully authenticates via SSO for the first time — no invitation required.

Enable it under **SSO settings → Just-in-time provisioning**. JIT-provisioned users are assigned the **Viewer** role by default. Change the default role and workspace assignment using the JIT settings fields.

<Callout type="info">
If you use SCIM for authoritative provisioning, disable JIT to avoid conflicts between SCIM-managed and JIT-provisioned accounts.
</Callout>

## Enforcing SSO

Once SSO is configured and tested, click **Enforce SSO** to require all users to sign in via the IdP. Email/password login is disabled for all non-admin users. Admins retain email/password access as a break-glass option.
