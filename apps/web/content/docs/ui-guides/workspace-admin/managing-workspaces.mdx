---
title: Creating and managing workspaces
description: New workspace setup, renaming, archiving, and per-workspace policy isolation.
---

Workspaces are isolated policy environments within your Spctre organisation. Each workspace has its own policy branches, bundles, users, and settings. Use workspaces to separate concerns: by team, product line, environment tier, or regulatory scope.

## Creating a workspace

<Steps>
<Step>

In the left nav, click **Admin → Workspaces**.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **New workspace**.

</Step>
<Step>

Fill in:
- **Name** — short identifier (e.g. `payments-prod`, `ml-platform`, `compliance-sandbox`). This becomes the workspace slug and appears in bundle paths.
- **Display name** — full label shown in the UI
- **Description** — optional; helps other admins understand the purpose

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Create workspace**. The workspace is created with no users and no policy branches. You're automatically added as an Admin.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Renaming a workspace

1. Go to **Admin → Workspaces**, click the workspace name.
2. Click **Settings** → **General**.
3. Update the **Display name** field. Note: the slug (URL-safe name used in API calls and bundle paths) cannot be changed after creation.

## Policy isolation

Each workspace has its own:
- Policy branches and published bundles
- User role assignments (someone can be a Reviewer in Workspace A and a Policy Creator in Workspace B)
- Settings (retention period, rate limit overrides, gateway mode)

Organisation-scope policies apply across all workspaces — these are authored in the `organisation` workspace scope. Workspace-scope policies only affect agents in that workspace.

## Archiving a workspace

Archiving removes a workspace from active use without deleting its history.

1. Go to the workspace **Settings → General**.
2. Click **Archive workspace**.
3. Confirm.

Archived workspaces are listed under **Admin → Workspaces → Archived**. Their policy history, audit trail, and compliance data are preserved. Archived workspaces cannot receive new branches or bundles. To restore, click **Unarchive**.

<Callout type="warn">
Deleting a workspace is permanent and removes all associated data. Archive instead if you might need the history.
</Callout>
