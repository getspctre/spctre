---
title: Compliance Officer overview
description: The compliance officer's view of Spctre — key screens and their purpose.
---

As a Compliance Officer, you use Spctre to read, verify, and export evidence of AI agent governance. You don't author policies or handle live escalations — your focus is on compliance posture, audit evidence, and providing attestation to external auditors.

## Your key screens

| Screen | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Compliance → Overview** | Packet readiness, governance posture, and the next action before audit handoff |
| **Compliance → Audit packet** | Packet contents, control mappings, verification detail, seals, and exports |
| **Compliance → Evidence & retention** | Policy lifecycle, linked evidence, and retention decisions |
| **Compliance → Delivery** | GRC delivery health and integration handoff status |
| **Compliance → Audit trail** | Immutable record of every decision, policy revision, and approval |
| **Audit → Audit Ledger** | Platform event log — logins, config changes, bundle publishes |
| **Cost → Cost & Usage** | Token and cost summaries for budget compliance |

## What you can do

| Action | Where |
|---|---|
| Determine whether a packet is ready | **Compliance → Overview** |
| Inspect and export the audit packet | **Compliance → Audit packet** |
| Generate a compliance seal | **Compliance → Audit packet** → **Generate seal** |
| Trace lifecycle and retention evidence | **Compliance → Evidence & retention** |
| Review GRC delivery health | **Compliance → Delivery** |
| Trace a specific decision | **Compliance → Audit trail** → search by decision ID |
| Read the audit ledger | **Audit → Audit Ledger** |
| Interpret the cost & usage dashboard | **Cost → Cost & Usage** |

## What you cannot do

Compliance Officers have read-only access to all governance data. You cannot author policies, approve branches, or resolve escalations. If you find a compliance gap that needs a policy fix, raise it with your Policy Creator or workspace admin.

## Where to start

If you're preparing for an audit, begin on **Compliance → Overview**. Resolve its highest-priority readiness check, then use **Audit packet** to seal or export the evidence. Use **Evidence & retention** when a reviewer needs the lifecycle record behind the packet.
