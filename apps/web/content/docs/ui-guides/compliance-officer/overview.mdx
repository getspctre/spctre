As a Compliance Officer, you use Spctre to read, verify, and export evidence of AI agent governance. You don't author policies or handle live escalations — your focus is on compliance posture, audit evidence, and providing attestation to external auditors.

## Your key screens

| Screen | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Compliance → Reports** | Aggregated compliance scores by connector, agent, and time period |
| **Compliance → Audit trail** | Immutable record of every decision, policy revision, and approval |
| **Audit → Audit Ledger** | Platform event log — logins, config changes, bundle publishes |
| **Cost → Cost & Usage** | Token and cost summaries for budget compliance |

## What you can do

| Action | Where |
|---|---|
| Read compliance reports | **Compliance → Reports** |
| Filter reports by date, connector, agent | Report filters |
| Export compliance data | **Export** button on any report |
| Generate a compliance seal | Report header → **Generate seal** |
| Trace a specific decision | **Compliance → Audit trail** → search by decision ID |
| Read the audit ledger | **Audit → Audit Ledger** |
| Interpret the cost & usage dashboard | **Cost → Cost & Usage** |

## What you cannot do

Compliance Officers have read-only access to all governance data. You cannot author policies, approve branches, or resolve escalations. If you find a compliance gap that needs a policy fix, raise it with your Policy Creator or workspace admin.

## Understanding the compliance score

The compliance score on the Reports page represents the percentage of agent decisions that were governed by an active policy and produced an evidence record — regardless of whether that decision was `ALLOW`, `DENY`, or `WARN`. A 100% score means every decision was covered. A score below 100% means some decisions happened without policy coverage (provenance gaps).

<Callout type="info">
A high WARN or DENY rate is not a compliance failure — it means your policies are catching risk. A provenance gap is the concern: decisions where no policy evaluated the action at all.
</Callout>
