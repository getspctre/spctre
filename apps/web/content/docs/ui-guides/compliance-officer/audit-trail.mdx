---
title: Investigating decision evidence
description: Find a recorded agent decision and inspect its rule and policy provenance.
---

Open **Audit → Decision evidence** to investigate recorded agent decisions. Policy publication, approval, and other administrative events are listed separately in **Change ledger**.

## Finding a decision

Choose **Search** to filter the evidence loaded on the current page by text, status, connector, runtime stack, branch, revision, or date. A new search has no implicit connector or status filter. Use **Reset** to clear the filters.

Branch and revision inputs suggest references from the loaded evidence. A branch name is shown when its provenance includes one. You can also enter an exact ID; revision suggestions narrow when you select a branch. These suggestions are not a catalog of every revision in the workspace.

## Inspecting a result

Choose **Inspect decision** on a search result. The same details used by the evidence table appear in the search panel:

- Recorded outcome, reason, agent, runtime, and environment.
- Decision ID, artifact hash, and evidence source.
- Policy references linking to the rule inventory.
- Policy context linking to the governing branch, with approval and publication metadata when recorded.
- Additional intent, parameters, and adapter metadata when available.

Choose **Back to search results** to return with your search fields intact. In the main evidence table, choose the action button to inspect a record with the keyboard, or click its row.

## Reading timestamps

Evidence timestamps include seconds and an explicit **UTC** timezone. Their presentation follows your interface language. Hover over a timestamp to see the precise ISO timestamp.

## Evidence limits

Inspect the information actually recorded for the decision. Missing policy context or provenance is displayed as missing; it is not proof that an approval occurred. Use the evidence export and the Change ledger when an investigation also requires administrative events.
