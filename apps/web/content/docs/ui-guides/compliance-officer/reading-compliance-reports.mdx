---
title: Working with an audit package
description: Check audit-package readiness, inspect the supporting proof, and hand off evidence.
---

The Audit package workspace is organised around an audit package: the published policy artifact, the decision evidence linked to it, and the checks required before external handoff. It answers: "Can we rely on this package, and where is the proof?"

## Start on Overview

1. In the left nav under **Audit**, click **Audit package**.
2. The **Overview** tab shows the package's readiness checks and governance posture.
3. Select the highlighted readiness check to open the exact supporting view.

## Check packet readiness

Overview checks five prerequisites for an external handoff:

| Check | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Published artifact** | A current policy revision is included in the packet |
| **Decision evidence** | The package links to recorded agent decisions |
| **Verification** | The artifact has a current passing verification result |
| **Retention** | No linked evidence record has expired |
| **Control mappings** | Published rules are linked to the controls being evidenced |

The primary action takes you to the first check that needs attention. When every check is ready, the page offers a JSON download for the current packet.

## Inspect the audit packet

Open the **Audit package** tab to review and export the auditor-facing artifact. It contains:

- the published artifact and its package sections;
- rule-to-control mappings and the linked runtime-evidence rollup;
- verification detail and compatibility drift; and
- options to seal the packet or download JSON and, when enabled, PDF.

Sealing records a durable audit event. Do this only after the readiness checks match the assurance you are providing.

## Trace evidence and retention

Open **Evidence & retention** when you need to investigate the proof behind a packet. It includes:

- the policy lifecycle timeline;
- the retention plan and rules; and
- individual evidence retention decisions.

Use the **Rules** and **Decisions** tabs within retention to switch between the policy that governs retention and the resulting evidence records.

## Review delivery

The **Delivery** tab shows whether configured GRC destinations are active and whether recent attempts need attention. Integration endpoints remain available there for authenticated administrators.
