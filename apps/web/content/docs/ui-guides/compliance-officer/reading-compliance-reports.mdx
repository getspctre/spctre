title: Reading a compliance report
description: Summary metrics, by-connector breakdown, by-agent breakdown, and understanding the compliance score.
---

The compliance report is the primary view of your organisation's governance posture over a period. It answers: "What did our AI agents do, and was every action governed?"

## Opening a report

1. In the left nav, click **Compliance → Reports**.
2. The report loads for the default time period (last 30 days). Change this with the date picker in the top-right.

## Summary metrics

The top row of the report shows four headline numbers:

| Metric | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Total decisions** | Every agent decision recorded in the period |
| **Compliance rate** | Percentage of decisions covered by a policy |
| **WARN rate** | Percentage of decisions that matched a WARN rule |
| **Escalation rate** | Percentage of decisions that triggered an escalation |

The compliance rate is the most important figure. A 100% compliance rate means every decision had policy coverage. Anything below 100% means some decisions were made without any policy evaluating them — see [Understanding provenance gaps](/help-docs/ui-guides/compliance-officer/understanding-provenance-gaps).

## By-connector breakdown

Below the summary, a table lists every connector that received agent traffic, with per-connector metrics:

- **Decision count** — how many decisions hit this connector
- **Compliance rate** — coverage rate for this connector
- **ALLOW / DENY / WARN / ESCALATE** — decision distribution
- **Unique agents** — how many distinct agents used this connector

Click a connector row to drill into decisions for that connector only.

## By-agent breakdown

Click the **Agents** tab to switch from a connector view to an agent-centric view. For each agent:

- **Agent ID and name**
- **Total decisions**
- **Compliance rate**
- **Last decision timestamp**

This view is useful for spotting agents with persistently low compliance rates — a signal that their policy bundle may be stale or missing connectors.

## Decision distribution chart

The timeline chart at the bottom shows decision volume and distribution over the selected period. Use it to:
- Spot anomalies (sudden spikes in DENY or WARN)
- Identify quiet periods that may indicate agent outages
- Correlate policy publish dates with decision rate changes

## Compliance timeline

Hover over any point on the timeline to see the compliance rate at that moment and any policy events (branch published, bundle updated) that occurred around that time.

## Control mappings

Click the **Control mappings** tab to see every rule-to-control mapping (e.g. SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001, EU AI Act) declared by your published policy, alongside a live evidence rollup for each mapped control:

- **Decision count** — how many decisions in the period matched a rule mapped to this control, with a breakdown of how many were denied or warned
- **Latest evidence** — the timestamp of the most recent decision that matched the control

A control with zero decisions has a mapping but no evidence yet proving it operated in the selected period. On the Evidence ledger, a decision's policy-ref chip links back to the control the matched rule satisfies.
