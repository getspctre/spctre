The pack library lists all governance packs available to your organisation — built-in packs maintained by Spctre, plus any community or private packs your team has published.

## Opening the pack library

1. In the left nav, click **Packs**.
2. The library opens with an overview of all available packs, grouped by category.

## What you see on a pack card

Each pack card shows:
- **Pack name and connector** — e.g. "Stripe Payments Governance" for the `stripe` connector
- **Risk level** — `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, or `HIGH`; colour-coded for quick scanning
- **Rule count** — number of rules included
- **Version** — current semver version
- **Compatibility** — which agent frameworks the pack is designed for (`CrewAI`, `LangChain`, `OpenAI Agents`, etc.)

## Filtering packs

Use the filter bar above the pack list to narrow by:
- **Connector** — `stripe`, `slack`, `github`, `openai`, `anthropic`, etc.
- **Risk level** — filter to HIGH packs to prioritise the most sensitive areas
- **Category** — `financial`, `communication`, `code`, `ai-model`
- **Framework compatibility** — match packs to your agent's framework

## Reading pack details

Click a pack name to open its detail panel. You'll see:

- **Description** — what governance problem this pack addresses
- **Included rules** — a table of every rule with its `effect` (`ALLOW`, `DENY`, `WARN`, `ESCALATE`), targeted actions, and connectors
- **Changelog** — version history with dates and summaries
- **Review requirements** — minimum approvals and reviewer roles required when this pack is used in a branch

<Callout type="info">
A `DENY` rule in a pack will block the matched action entirely. A `WARN` rule allows the action but creates an evidence record for review. An `ESCALATE` rule pauses the agent and waits for a human decision.
</Callout>

## Searching

Use the search bar at the top of the library to find packs by name, connector, or tag.
