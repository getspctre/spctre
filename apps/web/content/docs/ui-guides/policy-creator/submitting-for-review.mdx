---
title: Submitting a branch for review
description: Moving a DRAFT branch to IN_REVIEW, selecting reviewers, and adding a submission note.
---

Once you're satisfied with your rules, submit the branch to move it from `DRAFT` to `IN_REVIEW`. Reviewers are notified and can begin evaluating.

## Prerequisites

Before submitting, make sure:
- Your branch has at least one rule
- All rules have a `stable_rule_id`, `title`, and `effect`
- You've tested any conditions using the condition tester

<Callout type="warn">
You cannot add, edit, or delete rules while a branch is `IN_REVIEW`. If you need to make changes, ask a reviewer to reject the branch first.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the branch review page

Go to **Policy lifecycle → Policy branches**, select your branch in the table to open the inspector, and click the secondary **Review & publish** action.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review changes

Select the **Review changes** stage. Review the specific rule additions/removals and the historical impact analysis. Then click **Compose** in the Effective Policy header to stack your branch layers and compile the bundle.
</Step>

<Step>
### Request approvals

The approvals panel is also in **Review changes**. It details the required approval seats for your branch's environment scope. Reviewers can log in, inspect your changes, and provide their approval. The separate **Publish** stage shows readiness, verification, export, and the publish action.

If no approval workflow is configured, Spctre defaults to one approval in an
eligible reviewer's role (preferring Admin where available). This keeps the
implicit workflow satisfiable even as workspace membership changes. Configure
an approval workflow when separate Security, Platform, or other role lanes are
required; a configured workflow always takes precedence.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Reviewers can approve or reject the branch. Once the minimum approval threshold is reached and no one has rejected, the branch moves to `APPROVED`. The bundle does not go live until a publisher opens the **Publish** stage, clicks **Publish bundle**, and confirms the release; agents pick up the new bundle on their next sync after that publish.

If the branch is rejected, you'll receive a notification with the reviewer's feedback. See [Editing a branch after feedback](/help-docs/ui-guides/policy-creator/editing-after-feedback) for next steps.
