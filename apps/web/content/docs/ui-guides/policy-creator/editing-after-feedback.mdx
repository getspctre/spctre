---
title: Editing a branch after feedback
description: Responding to reviewer comments, making changes, and re-submitting.
---

When a reviewer rejects your branch, the branch moves back to `DRAFT` and you'll receive a notification. The rejection includes structured feedback that you respond to before re-submitting.

## Finding reviewer feedback

1. Go to **Policy lifecycle → Policy branches**.
2. Click your branch to open the branch inspector slideout panel.
3. Click **Review & Publish** to open the review surface, then click the **Approve** tab to see all comments.

## Reading the feedback

Each piece of feedback is attached to a specific rule or to the branch overall. Comments show:
- The reviewer's name and timestamp
- The specific concern (e.g. "This condition allows charges above the tier limit for premium accounts")
- A suggested action if the reviewer left one

## Making changes

With the branch back in `DRAFT`, you can:

- **Edit a rule** — click **Author rules** from the branch inspector, click the rule row, and modify it.
- **Delete a rule** — on the **Rule Authoring** page, click the rule row and select **Remove rule** in the edit panel.
- **Add a rule** — on the **Rule Authoring** page, click **Add rule** at the bottom of the list.
- **Re-import** — upload a revised policy file via **Create policy → Upload or paste existing** on the Policies page.

<Callout type="info">
When you edit a rule that a reviewer left a comment on, the comment is preserved and shown in context. Reviewers can see what changed between submissions.
</Callout>

## Responding to comments

Click **Reply** on a reviewer comment to add your response. This is visible to reviewers in the next review cycle. Use replies to explain the decision you made — if you chose not to act on a suggestion, say why.

You can mark a comment as **Resolved** once you've addressed it. Resolved comments are collapsed but not deleted.

## Re-submitting

When you're done making changes:

1. Click **Submit for review** again.
2. The same reviewers receive a notification.
3. They see a diff view showing exactly what changed since the previous submission.
4. The branch re-enters `IN_REVIEW`.

Reviewers can approve or reject again. Previous approvals are **reset** on re-submission — all configured reviewers must approve again.

## Abandoning changes

If you decide the feedback requires a fundamentally different approach, you can discard the branch and start fresh. See [Managing draft branches](/help-docs/ui-guides/policy-creator/managing-drafts).
