---
title: Setting up alerting for your queue
description: Connecting Slack or PagerDuty so new escalations and reviews notify you immediately.
---

By default, queue items appear in the Spctre control plane UI. Configure alerting so that new escalations page you immediately — especially important for escalations with short SLA windows.

## Opening alerting settings

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Settings → Alerting**.

## Available channels

| Channel | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Non-urgent reviews; lower volume |
| **Slack** | Team channels; quick collaborative triage |
| **PagerDuty** | On-call rotations; escalation SLA alerting |
| **Generic webhook** | Custom integrations (Teams, Opsgenie, etc.) |
| **Splunk HEC** | Rule-triggered DENY/violation forwarding to Splunk |
| **Microsoft Sentinel** | Rule-triggered DENY/violation forwarding to Sentinel |

## Configuring Slack

<Steps>
<Step>

Click **Add channel → Slack**.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Connect to Slack**. You'll be redirected to Slack to authorise the Spctre app for your workspace.

</Step>
<Step>

After authorising, return to Spctre and choose the Slack channel (or DM) to send alerts to.

</Step>
<Step>

Under **Alert rules**, choose which events trigger a Slack message:
- New branch assigned to me
- Branch re-submitted after rejection
- New escalation assigned to me
- Escalation SLA warning (configurable threshold, e.g. 50% of SLA remaining)

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Save**. Send a test alert to confirm delivery.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Configuring PagerDuty

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add channel → PagerDuty**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter your PagerDuty **Integration Key** (from a PagerDuty Events API v2 integration).
</Step>
<Step>
Set **Severity mapping**:
- HIGH risk escalation → `critical`
- MEDIUM risk escalation → `error`
- LOW risk escalation → `warning`
</Step>
<Step>
Enable **Escalation SLA alert** and set the threshold (e.g. alert when 30 minutes remain).
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Alert rules

Each alert rule has:
- **Trigger** — what event fires it
- **Filter** — optional scope (e.g. only HIGH risk, only connector `stripe`)
- **Channel** — where to send it

Add multiple rules to route different alert types to different channels — for example, branch reviews to a Slack channel and escalations to PagerDuty.

## Testing

After saving, click **Send test alert** next to any channel. A synthetic alert is sent using the same format as a real alert.
