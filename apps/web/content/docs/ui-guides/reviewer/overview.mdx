---
title: Reviewer overview
description: The Reviewer role — what lands in your queue and why.
---

As a Reviewer, you evaluate policy branches submitted by Policy Creators and decide whether they're safe and correct to publish. You also handle escalations — live decisions that agents have paused on and sent to you for a human call.

## Two queues

| Queue | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Review queue** | Policy branches in `IN_REVIEW` status |
| **Escalation queue** | Live agent decisions paused with `ESCALATE` effect |

Both are accessible from the left nav under **Review**.

## What you can do

| Action | Where |
|---|---|
| Review a policy branch | **Review → Branches** |
| Approve or reject a branch | Branch detail → **Approve / Reject** |
| Work the WARN record queue | **Audit Log** |
| Mark WARN records as acknowledged | Audit Log record → **Acknowledge** |
| Handle escalations | **Review → Escalations** |
| Reassign an escalation | Escalation detail → **Reassign** |
| Configure queue alerting | **Settings → Alerting** |

## Reviewing a policy revision

The Review workspace follows one explicit path: inspect **Review changes**, assess the **Impact summary**, complete **Approvals & readiness**, then publish. The page always identifies the next required step.

Artifact tools download the revision currently shown in Review. The separate **latest published bundle** download represents what is deployed now and can be a different revision.

Restoring a prior revision republishes it to agents. Spctre requires an explicit production-change acknowledgement before that restore is submitted.

## What you cannot do

Reviewers cannot author or edit policy branches — that's the Policy Creator's role. Approval also does not publish the bundle. Once the minimum approval threshold is reached, the branch moves to `APPROVED`; a user with publish authority must release it manually from the **Publish** stage.

## How you're assigned

Policy Creators select you by name or email when submitting a branch. For escalations, assignment is based on the escalation routing rules configured in the workspace — either round-robin, a specific role, or a named individual.

## Notification channels

By default, you receive in-app notifications. Configure Slack, PagerDuty, or email alerts in **Settings → Alerting** so new items in your queue reach you immediately.

## Responding to time pressure

Escalations have an SLA countdown. When the countdown expires, the escalation auto-resolves with a `TIMEOUT` decision (configurable per workspace — typically `DENY` for safety). Prioritise escalations over branch reviews.
