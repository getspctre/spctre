title: Reviewing a policy branch
description: Reading the diff view, checking rule intent, and understanding semantic checks.
---

When a Policy Creator submits a branch, it enters your review queue. Your job is to verify that the rules are correct, safe, and match the stated intent before approving publication.

## Opening a branch for review

1. Go to **Policy lifecycle → Policy branches** in the left nav.
2. Under the **Branches** table, click the branch to open the inspector slideout panel.
3. Click the secondary **Review & Publish** button.

## What you see

### Header

- **Branch name** — what the creator intended to govern
- **Publish Button** — Direct header button to release the approved policy bundle to production when approvals are satisfied
- **Workspace, scope, and active revision**
- **Required approvals** — how many are needed; how many are complete

### Review changes stage

This stage contains:
- **Diff view** — compares rules in the branch against the parent baseline (added, modified, removed rules).
- **Impact Analysis** — maps the rule changes to historical logs to assess risk.
- **Compose (Build)** — stacks branch layers (organization, workspace, environment, connector) to output a compiled bundle artifact with a unique `artifactHash`.
- **Approvals Panel** — where reviewers submit their role-based approvals.

### Publish stage

This stage contains:
- **Publish readiness** — shows blockers and the publish action for eligible publishers.
- **Compatibility Report** — lists validation checks against active agents.
- **Verification** — shows AGT verification results when available.
- **Export** — provides the bundle artifact.

### History stage

This stage contains the revision log for auditing and rollbacks.

## Checking rule intent

For each rule, ask:
1. Does the effect match the stated business requirement?
2. Do the conditions scope the rule appropriately, or could it match unintended decisions?
3. Is the priority correctly set relative to other rules in this workspace?
4. If `ESCALATE` — is there a reviewer configured and available for this connector?

## Leaving comments

Click **Comment** on any rule to leave structured feedback. Comments are visible to the creator and other reviewers. Use the optional **Suggest action** dropdown to indicate what you want changed.

## Next step

After reviewing, you [approve](/help-docs/ui-guides/reviewer/approving-a-branch) or [reject](/help-docs/ui-guides/reviewer/rejecting-a-branch) the branch.
