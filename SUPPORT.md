# Support

## Community support

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/getspctre/spctre/discussions) for
installation help, integration questions, and ideas that are not yet concrete
bug reports or feature proposals.

Use GitHub Issues for reproducible bugs and scoped feature requests. Check
existing discussions and issues first, and include the relevant Spctre version,
runtime, and deployment details.

## Security reports

Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report them privately.

## Commercial and hosted support

Hosted and Enterprise support are outside this public repository. Contact your
Spctre account team through the channel provided with your agreement.
